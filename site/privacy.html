Privacy

What happens when you subscribe to the Mosaix Format mailing list.

What we collect

Your email address, and nothing else. The form sends it to an automation service (n8n) that stores it in Resend, a transactional email platform. No cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels run on this site.

Double opt-in

After you submit the form, we send a confirmation email with a link. Your address is not active until you click that link. If you do not confirm, the address is deleted automatically and no further email is sent.

What we send

Two kinds of messages, and nothing else:

  • A notice when a new version of the specification ships (1.1, 2.0, etc.).
  • A notice when the checker is published as a pip package.

No newsletters, no promotions, no third-party content.

Who has access

Andrea Fiorino (the maintainer) and the Resend platform. Your address is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than the two messages above.

How to unsubscribe

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also write to hello@mosaixformat.org and ask to be removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@mosaixformat.org. If you are in the EU, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection). The data controller is Andrea Fiorino, Boom Digital, Verona, Italy.
