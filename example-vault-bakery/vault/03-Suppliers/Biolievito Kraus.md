# Biolievito Kraus

Biolievito Kraus is a one-person operation that maintains and distributes sourdough starter cultures. The bakery has used the same culture since opening day in 2019; it is refreshed in-house three times a week. ✅ sourced (purchase receipt BK-2019-04).

The culture's age is disputed: 40 years per the producer's website, 35 years per Layla's notes from a conversation with the founder — see [[Open questions]] #3. The starter is the base of the [[Rye country loaf]] and adds flavour to the ciabatta pre-ferment.
