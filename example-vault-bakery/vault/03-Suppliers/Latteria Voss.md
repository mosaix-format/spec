# Latteria Voss

Latteria Voss is a small dairy about 45 km north, running a herd of 120 Braunvieh cows. Supplies 84 % butter and fresh cream, delivered every Monday morning. ✅ sourced (supply agreement LV-2020-03).

The butter goes into the [[Almond croissant]] lamination and the tart bases. Price: €8.60/kg for butter, stable since 2023. Minimum order: 10 kg per week.
