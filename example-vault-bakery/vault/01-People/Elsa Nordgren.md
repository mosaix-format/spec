# Elsa Nordgren

Elsa Nordgren (born 1986) trained in pastry at a culinary school in Lyon, then worked in two bakeries before co-founding Forno Vialetto in 2019 with [[Marco Trevisan]] and [[Layla Beshir]]. She sets the product range each quarter and runs the customer-facing side of the shop. ✅ sourced (partnership agreement, 2019-03).

The house bread, [[Ciabatta Vialetto]], is her recipe. She holds a 40 % share of the business.
