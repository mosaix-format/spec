# Marco Trevisan

Marco Trevisan (born 1983) is a former construction worker who moved into baking after apprenticing at a village oven in the mountains. He co-founded Forno Vialetto in 2019 with [[Elsa Nordgren]] and [[Layla Beshir]]. He rebuilt [[The wood-fired oven]] in summer 2020 and runs [[Baking shifts]] starting at 3:30 a.m., Tuesday to Saturday. ✅ sourced (partnership agreement, 2019-03).

He holds a 30 % share of the business.
