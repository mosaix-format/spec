# Layla Beshir

Layla Beshir (born 1991) studied food science before joining the bakery as the third partner in 2019. She manages the relationships with all three suppliers: [[Mulino Cerrina]], [[Latteria Voss]] and [[Biolievito Kraus]]. She develops new recipes and keeps tasting notes on every batch. ✅ sourced (partnership agreement, 2019-03).

She holds a 30 % share of the business and leads sourcing decisions together with [[Elsa Nordgren]] and [[Marco Trevisan]].
