# Open questions

## #1 · Flour price from Mulino Cerrina

- Version A — March 2025, source: quote MC-2025-03. €0.85/kg for type-1 flour.
- Version B — June 2025, source: invoice MC-2025-06-014. €0.92/kg for the same flour.

Consequence: the ciabatta cost model changes by €0.04 per loaf; margin drops below 60 % if B is the new baseline.
Owner: [[Mulino Cerrina]] contact · status: open.

## #2 · Saturday market pitch fee

- Version A — 2024, source: verbal agreement with market manager. €120/month.
- Version B — January 2025, source: new contract draft. €150/month.

Consequence: annual market cost rises by €360; changes the break-even from 18 to 22 loaves per Saturday.
Owner: Marco Trevisan · see [[Saturday market at Piazza Solari]] · status: open.

## #3 · Age of the sourdough starter

- Version A — [[Biolievito Kraus]] website, accessed 2025-04. Claims the starter culture is 40 years old.
- Version B — [[Layla Beshir]]'s tasting notes, 2024-11. Records 35 years, told by the founder in person.

Consequence: marketing materials and the shop sign say "40-year starter"; if B is correct, the claim is overstated.
Owner: Layla Beshir · status: open.
