# Panificio Reale

Panificio Reale has been in the neighbourhood since 1998. It sells 12 types of bread (vs. our two), focaccia, pizza al taglio and a small range of biscuits. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 7:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and 4:00 to 7:30 p.m. ✅ sourced (observation, 2025-02).

Three wholesale accounts with local restaurants, a channel Forno Vialetto has not entered. See [[Viale Giardini neighbourhood]]. The owner has been there for 27 years; local trust is high.
