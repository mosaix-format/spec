# Unsold bread policy

All unsold bread and pastries are collected by a neighbourhood food bank at 1:30 p.m. each closing day. The arrangement has been in place since the bakery opened in 2019. ✅ sourced (food bank agreement, 2019-05).

Average daily surplus: about 8 % of production (2024 figures). The surplus is higher on rainy days and lower on Saturdays (market absorbs the extra). The policy aligns with the bakery's decision to stay [[Closed on Mondays]] — no stale bread carried over.
