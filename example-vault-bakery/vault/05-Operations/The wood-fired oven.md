# The wood-fired oven

Brick oven built on a 1960s base that was part of the premises when the bakery moved in. [[Marco Trevisan]] rebuilt the dome and the flue in summer 2020, keeping the original hearth slab. Capacity: 28 loaves per load. ✅ sourced (renovation invoice, 2020-08).

Fuel: seasoned beechwood, about 15 kg per baking day, sourced from a local firewood dealer at €180 per tonne. The oven is central to the flavour of the [[Ciabatta Vialetto]] — the crust character does not replicate in an electric oven (tested in 2021).
