# Organic certification

Elsa Nordgren raised the idea in February 2025. [[Mulino Cerrina]] already offers organic-certified flour at a 12 % premium. [[Latteria Voss]] could certify within six months. [[Biolievito Kraus]] is already organic. ⚠ to confirm (internal discussion, 2025-02).

Estimated cost increase: 15 % on ingredients, or about €0.35 per loaf. The partners have not decided; [[Elsa Nordgren]] favours it, Marco is cautious about the paperwork. A decision is expected by September 2025.
