# Hazelnut and honey tart

Revised version of the [[Hazelnut tart (old recipe)]], introduced in January 2024. Replaces cane sugar with chestnut honey (from a local apiary) and adds a pinch of sea salt to balance the sweetness. Developed by [[Layla Beshir]] after three months of customer feedback. ✅ sourced (recipe log, 2024-01).

Retail price: €4.50 per slice. Available autumn and winter only (honey supply is seasonal). Sells about 15 slices per day in season.
