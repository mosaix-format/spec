# Rye country loaf

A 70/30 rye-wheat loaf, proofed for 18 hours with the sourdough starter from [[Biolievito Kraus]] and baked once daily at 6:00 a.m. Dense, moist crumb with a thick flour-dusted crust. Rye flour from [[Mulino Cerrina]], milled to order every two weeks. ✅ sourced (recipe log, 2020-01).

Retail price: €5.20 per 750 g loaf. Sells best at [[Saturday market at Piazza Solari]], where it accounts for about 40 % of market-day revenue.
