# Ciabatta Vialetto

High-hydration ciabatta (78 % hydration) with a thin, crackling crust and an open, irregular crumb. Developed by [[Elsa Nordgren]] in 2019 using type-1 flour from [[Mulino Cerrina]]. Baked twice daily in [[The wood-fired oven]], first batch at 5:30 a.m., second at 11:00 a.m. ✅ sourced (recipe log, 2019-06).

Retail price: €3.80 per 500 g loaf. Accounts for about 35 % of daily revenue. The flour costs approximately €0.85–0.92/kg depending on the current quote.
