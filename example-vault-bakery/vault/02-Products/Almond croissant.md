# Almond croissant

Laminated croissant with almond frangipane filling and flaked-almond topping, baked fresh each morning. The only viennoiserie on the menu, introduced by [[Elsa Nordgren]] in 2020. Uses high-fat butter (84 %) from [[Latteria Voss]], which gives the dough its colour and flake. ✅ sourced (recipe log, 2020-04).

Retail price: €3.20 each. Batch size: 40 pieces. Usually sells out by 10:00 a.m.
