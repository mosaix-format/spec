# §8 Out of scope

Not defined: how summary and keys are generated; how documents become notes; how notes are retrieved; how composed documents are edited; how the vault is exposed to agents or synced; how the graph is drawn. A vault produced by hand can be fully conformant ([[§10 Conformance]]).
