# §12 Provenance

The two-axis reliability model, the open-questions ledger and supersede-don't-delete come from working with sources of different dates. The composed-document type comes from a vault of good atomic notes being unreadable by the people who needed it. Earlier working name: [[SLIM Vault Format]].
