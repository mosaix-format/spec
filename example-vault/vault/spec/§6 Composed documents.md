# §6 Composed documents

Atomic notes are for machines; people read documents. A note with `type: document` lists `fragments` in reading order, optionally `pool` and `layout`. The fragments stay separate; the document is the view ([[R5 Composition over duplication]]). Example in this vault: [[Mosaix for a new team]].
