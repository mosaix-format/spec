# Mosaix — identity

**Mark.** Four irregular tesserae with the grout forming an x; the fourth, outlined and empty, is the tile still to confirm. File: `mosaix-mark.svg`. One colour, `currentColor`.

**Favicon.** The same tiles with narrower grout and a thicker outline, so that the empty tile stays visible at 16 px. File: `mosaix-favicon.svg`.

**Wordmark.** The mark followed by the full word "mosaix", lowercase, IBM Plex Sans 600. File: `mosaix-wordmark.svg`.

**Animated marks.** `anim/mosaix-loader.svg` (the four tiles settling) and `anim/mosaix-compose.svg` (a document unpacking into tiles). SMIL, one colour, no dependencies.

**Type.** IBM Plex, SIL Open Font License 1.1: Plex Sans 600 for the wordmark and headings, Plex Serif 400/600 for text, Plex Mono 400/500 for code. Self-hosted in `site/fonts/`.

**Colour.** Paper `#efeae0`, secondary paper `#e4ded1`, ink `#14130f`, secondary ink `#4a4741`, muted `#615c53`, rule `#d2cbbb`. One functional accent, gold `#c9a227`: it marks what is being composed or is still open (fragments of a document, entries to confirm). It is never used for the mark itself, which is always a single colour.

**Do not.** Coloured tiles, gradients, different opacities between tiles (it reads as another well-known mark), an x made of two tiles only (it does not read), "AI" in the name or in the sign, rounded corners anywhere.

**Use by others.** The mark, favicon and wordmark may be reproduced unaltered to refer to the format, for example next to a conformance claim. They are not licensed as the identity of another product or service (see `../LICENSE.md`).
